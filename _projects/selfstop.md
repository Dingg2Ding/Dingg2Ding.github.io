## **Overview**

SelfStop is an ongoing research app for studying self-regulation during short-form video viewing on Android and iOS. It hosts Reels and Shorts in an in-app WebView and supports different viewing and intervention conditions.

## **What I Contributed**

My work focuses on platform integration and data collection development within the project.

- Supported the integration of short-form video viewing and intervention flows across Android and iOS.
- Worked on logging viewing time, video entry events, and intervention events.
- Worked on local event storage and retryable synchronization with Supabase.

## **Research App Scope**

The app supports Baseline, Black, and Live study conditions. Viewing and intervention logs support the study's data collection needs.

## **Current Status**

Development and validation are ongoing. The Black intervention and return-to-viewing flow have been reviewed in an iOS demonstration video. Full physical-device validation and study deployment are not yet complete.
